## Prompt voor voorwerpen

Generate one educational photographic asset sheet, landscape exactly 3:2, white background, strict grid of 4 equal columns and 3 equal rows, twelve isolated objects. Each object centered well inside its own cell with generous white space, no gridlines, no labels, no text, no numbering, no watermark. Photorealistic studio product photographs, sharp, child-friendly, very subtle shadows. Row 1 left to right: a small toy house viewed straight from front with triangular red roof, rectangular white facade and two square windows; a toy delivery truck viewed exactly from side with rectangular white cargo box and two clearly circular black wheels; a round-faced red alarm clock viewed from front; an empty tan waffle ice cream cone on its side with its open circular mouth visible. Row 2 left to right: a basketball alone; a plain unbranded silver cylindrical drinks can; a blue rectangular tissue box without tissues; a small solid wooden square-base pyramid, perspective shows triangular faces. Row 3 left to right: one perfectly round plain cookie viewed straight from above; one blank rectangular white postcard with thin gray outline viewed straight from above; one perfectly square yellow sticky note viewed straight from above, flat no curled corners; a flat triangular cheese slice viewed straight from above with a clear triangular outline. Preserve exact object order and strict equal cell placement. No object crosses the cell margins.

## Prompt voor samengestelde figuren

Create a clean educational PNG asset sheet on pure white background, landscape 3:2 ratio. Strict 2 columns by 2 rows, each cell contains one large colorful figure assembled ONLY from simple flat geometric shapes, generous whitespace around every figure, no labels, no text, no decorative extra details, no background, no shadows. Exact figures: TOP LEFT a fish made of ONE large blue circle body and ONE orange triangle tail on its left, ONE tiny black circular eye inside the body. TOP RIGHT a rocket pointing up made of ONE tall blue rectangle body, ONE red triangle nose above, TWO orange triangles side fins below left and right, ONE yellow circle window in middle. BOTTOM LEFT a house made of ONE orange square wall, ONE large red triangle roof, ONE tall blue rectangular door, TWO small blue square windows. BOTTOM RIGHT a robot made of ONE green square head, ONE large blue rectangle torso, TWO orange thin rectangles arms, TWO orange thin rectangles legs, TWO tiny black circular eyes. Shapes have thin clean dark outlines. All shapes are mathematically clean with flat solid colors, no rounding of square corners. Child-friendly recognizable silhouettes. Keep exact 2x2 grid order. This is for a school worksheet identifying flat shapes.
