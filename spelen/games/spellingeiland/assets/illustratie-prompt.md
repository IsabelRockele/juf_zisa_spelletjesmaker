# Eilandenwereld

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool, op 26 september 2026.
Projectbestand: `eilandenwereld.png`.
De naambordjes zijn in HTML toegevoegd, zodat ze leesbaar en aanklikbaar blijven.

## Gebruikte prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: polished full-width illustrated world map background for a children's educational iPad game, landscape 1536x1024. Create a beautiful richly detailed, charming storybook 3D-painted archipelago in vivid turquoise-blue water, a cohesive real game world viewed from a high isometric camera. Exactly THREE large distinct lush islands with sandy natural coastlines surrounded and separated by plenty of visible sparkling ocean. Composition for clickable map: spelling island in LEFT third centered at (25% width, 45% height); mathematics island in RIGHT third centered at (74%, 43%); puzzle island in LOWER CENTER centered at (51%, 77%). Keep island silhouettes distinct and all entirely in frame. Upper 15% mostly open pale blue sky/water for a heading overlay; bottom 5% open water. Spelling island: charming cottage shaped like an open book, whimsical tower made of alphabet blocks showing a b c, big upright pencil, ink quill, little books, winding path and leafy trees. Mathematics island: playful number house, large colorful wooden abacus, a clock tower, chunky 1 2 3 numbers and plus/minus symbols as park sculptures, a measuring ruler bridge over a little stream. Puzzle island: interlocking colorful puzzle-shaped garden areas, a little maze hedge, geometric wooden blocks and a tiny jewel chest. All islands lush and explorable, rounded cozy architecture, flowers, rocks, palm trees, wooden docks, toy-like depth and soft warm sunshine. Water with clear shallow turquoise halos around each island, gentle white wave strokes, one tiny sailboat between islands and dotted subtle sailing routes. Premium children's adventure game illustration, meticulous readable detail, warm saturated balanced colors, tactile painterly surfaces, beautiful inviting lighting. Not a dashboard, not a card layout, no panels, no borders, no emojis, no UI, no written island titles, no logos, no watermark. Letters and numbers only as physical educational props on their islands. Plenty of breathing room so each island can be tapped separately. The finished image itself is the navigable world.
