## Gebruikte prompt

Create a polished game sprite PNG on a genuinely transparent background. Use the turquoise pickup truck at top right of reference as exact vehicle design reference, not the buggy or sports car. ONE truck only, seen directly FROM BEHIND, camera slightly elevated so we see inside empty pickup bed, back of cabin, tailgate, rear bumper and chunky tyres. Symmetric straight rear chase-camera view, modest perspective, NOT side view, NOT top down. Same rounded turquoise body, dark tyres gold rims, realistic friendly animated-film finish. Two red rear lights, small ivory license plate with a simple dark 7. Full car isolated, tight centered framing with 5% transparent margin, no ground, no landscape, no words, no front face. This asset will be the player's car in a child-friendly pseudo-3D driving game.

## Spel

De baan wordt in perspectief getekend. De auto is een PNG-sprite; de race is geen vrij bestuurbare 3D-wereld. Sturen verplaatst de auto binnen de baan, met begrenzing aan beide randen. Gas, rem, versnellingsstroken, botsingen, computertegenstanders en de finish worden in het spel berekend.
